II. Infections Treat by goals

Pneumonia
Often atypical: confusion, anorexia, falls, lethargy. RR >24 + SpO₂ <92% off baseline are the most reliable signs. Treatment is a goals conversation, not a microbiology problem.
Cellulitis
Empiric outpatient PO works in ~90%. Mark the border; reassess 24 + 48 h. Don't culture skin.
Cystitis / UTI
Most positive cultures are asymptomatic bacteriuria — do NOT treat. Confusion in dementia rarely means UTI. Falls alone: look for orthostasis, deconditioning, polypharmacy first.
