IPU PRN dosing

Alive hospice inpatient unit · opioid, benzodiazepine and haloperidol range orders
Companion to the Alive IPU PRN Range Orders policy (v2.0, draft for P&T review, 17 September 2026) and the Assessment Scales Reference. The printed doses, bands, reassessment intervals and notification triggers reproduce those documents and must not be edited here. Local formulary availability and EMR dose-entry controls take precedence. This tool holds no patient data; the text it produces is pasted into the record by the clinician, who remains responsible for its accuracy.
